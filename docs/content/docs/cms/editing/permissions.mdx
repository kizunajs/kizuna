title: Who can do what
description: 'What an editor sees and may change depends on their role and, with several sites, on where they belong.'

## On one site

Every editor sees everything. A field's `auth` says who may change it, and `requireReview` says who must look before it goes live.

| Role     | Writes                     | Publishes                     |
| -------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| `editor` | Every field without `auth` | Pages without `requireReview` |
| `admin`  | Every field                | Everything                    |

A field an editor may not change shows greyed out, and the model gets the same answer: `403`. [Access](/docs/cms/editing/access) sets up who reaches what, down to one page or one item.

## With several sites

Inside a site, the rules above apply as they are. Shared content reaches every site, so an admin over every site publishes it. Pick who is looking:

| Editor                  | Shared content              | Their site              | Other sites       |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------- |
| `admin` over every site | Write and publish           | Write and publish       | Write and publish |
| `admin` on a site       | Draft, and ask for a review | As `admin` on one site  | Not shown         |
| `editor` on a site      | Draft, and ask for a review | As `editor` on one site | Not shown         |
| `editor` with no site   | Draft, and ask for a review | None                    | Not shown         |

A site's editor never sees another site, in the admin, the API or MCP. Roles and sites come from your identity, and [Sites](/docs/cms/sites#editors) shows the config.
