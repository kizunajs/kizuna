**Ask for review** sits in the `…` menu beside Publish. Pick one or more people, with the page's owner first and each person's role beside their name, and add a note for them. Send request waits until someone is picked:

<EditorFigure kind="ask" />

A message says "Asked Kari Nordmann to review", and its **Undo** takes the request back. The status then reads "Waiting for review by Kari Nordmann", with when you asked beside it, and each changed field carries a dot after its label. The reviewer sees the request in the editor, in their chat, and wherever you send it with `onReviewRequested`.

While it waits, **Withdraw request** in the `…` menu takes it back, and the page reads as if nobody was asked.

When Kari sends it back, the status reads "Sent back by Kari Nordmann". Her note and her comments sit once at the top of the form, each comment naming the field it is on, which a click shows. **Resolve** clears a comment once it is dealt with, and **Ask Kari again** in the header sends the draft back to her.

Once she approves, the page is live, and the header reads "Approved 2 min ago by Kari Nordmann".

<Tab value="Reviewing">

A page waiting for your review opens on its **Changes** tab, between Edit and Versions, with the number of changed fields beside it. The note from whoever asked comes first. Then each changed field shows locked, with what is live over what the draft holds: the words it lost struck out on the Live line, and the words it gained marked on the Draft line. The fields the draft leaves alone fold into one line, like "Button and Delivery note, unchanged". On the Edit tab, the same fields carry a dot after their label.

The header holds the answer, in Publish's place:

- **Approve and publish** approves the draft and publishes it in one step. Both happen, or neither does, and it is refused when the draft changed since you looked or someone else answered first.
- **Send back** asks what the person who asked should change, and sends your note to them with your comments.

Someone who may not publish the page, like a site's editor on [shared content](/docs/cms/sites), sees **Approve** instead, and someone who may publishes it afterwards. An approval covers the draft as it was, and a save after it marks the approval outdated. **Undo approval** takes it back while the draft is still unpublished, and the review waits for an answer again.

## Comments on a field

**Comment** beside a change on the Changes tab leaves a note on that one field, under the change. Enter adds it. The person who asked reads it at the top of the form once the draft is sent back. Either of them can comment, and **Resolve** clears a comment once it is dealt with. Comments stay with the draft when Kari is asked again, and publishing clears them.

## Requiring a review

```ts title="src/app/investor-relations/content.ts"
export default definePage({
    name: 'investorRelationsPage',
    requireReview: true,
    fields: [
        // ...
    ],
});
```

Publishing waits for an approval from someone other than the person who changed it. Anyone with a role under `reviews.roles` may approve without being asked. The rule holds in the routes, so the model gets the same answer.

## Publishing past a review

Someone with a role under `reviews.roles` can publish before the approval comes. The Publish dialog shows them **Publish anyway** beside the reason Publish is waiting, and asks once more before it goes live:

```ts title="kizuna.cms.config.ts"
reviews: {
    roles: 'admin',
},
```

The publish route takes `{ force: true }` for the same thing. For anyone without one of those roles, `force` changes nothing and the route still answers `409`.

The review records that the draft was published past it, and by whom. Versions shows that version as "Published past review" with their name, so it is clear afterwards that nobody approved it.

## People

The editor shows people by name and picture, and their roles where you pick reviewers. The admin's account menu shows your email under your name when the list gives one. `people.list` reads them from your own users:

```ts title="kizuna.cms.config.ts"
auth: {
    identity: 'editor',
    people: {
        list: async () => {
            const editors = await db
                .select()
                .from(users)
                .where(inArray(users.role, ['editor', 'admin']));
            return editors.map((editor) => ({
                id: editor.email,
                name: editor.name,
                email: editor.email,
                image: editor.image ?? undefined,
                roles: [editor.role],
            }));
        },
        imageOrigins: ['https://lh3.googleusercontent.com'],
    },
},
```

With [sites](/docs/cms/sites), give each person `sites` too, their role on each site like `{ uk: 'editor' }`. On a site's document, the people list and the reviewers hold only the people who hold that site, and the admins over every site. On shared content, they hold the people who share a site with you, and those admins. Overview's [`editors` widget](/docs/cms/dashboard#the-overview) names each person's site role beside their changes.

## Owners

A page, global or item can have an owner. The admin shows them under the document's **Info**, where an editor picks another, and the owner is suggested first for a review.

## Sending the request

```ts title="kizuna.cms.config.ts"
reviews: {
    onReviewRequested: async ({ documents, reviewers, requestedBy, note }, { plugins }) => {
        await plugins.resend.sendEmail({
            to: reviewers.map((reviewer) => reviewer.email),
            subject: `${requestedBy.name} asked you to review ${documents.length} pages`,
            text: note ?? '',
        });
    },
},
```
