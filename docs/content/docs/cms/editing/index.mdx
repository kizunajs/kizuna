---
title: The editor
description: 'Editors change content in their chat, or by clicking the page. Nothing goes live until someone publishes.'
---

Every change is a draft until a person publishes it. Try it as Sam, who runs the UK site: change the Privacy Policy's email and ask for a review, then sign in as Kim to publish it.

<EditingDemo />

Editors work in their chat, on the page and in the [admin](/docs/cms/editing/admin), and all three save to the same draft. To connect a chat, add the CMS's [MCP endpoint](/docs/mcp#showing-a-view) to Claude or another host with MCP Apps, signed in as an editor.

<Tabs items={['In the chat', 'On the page', 'Publishing']}>

<Tab value="In the chat">

<EditorFigure kind="chat" />

The editor opens beside the model's answer and fills in as it writes. What you type saves a moment after you stop, and the model sees it.

</Tab>

<Tab value="On the page">

Expand the editor and the site's draft shows beside the form. Click any text or image, and the form jumps to that field.

<EditorFigure kind="preview" />

Browse uses the page as a visitor does, and the form follows each link.

</Tab>

<Tab value="Publishing">

Publish lists every change and asks you to confirm. Asking the model to publish asks in the host, like any tool that [needs approval](/docs/mcp#asking-before-it-runs).

<EditorFigure kind="publish" />

</Tab>

</Tabs>
